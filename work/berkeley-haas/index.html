Leadership / Visiting lecturer

UC Berkeley Haas

Bringing growth and innovation into the classroom

RoleVisiting lecturer

PeriodJanuary 2021 – December 2024

FocusLeadership

Jen’s work has also included teaching the disciplines behind company building. As a visiting lecturer at UC Berkeley’s Haas School of Business, she delivered workshops on growth, innovation, go-to-market, startups, entrepreneurship, and impact. The role brought her operating and advisory experience into an educational setting over a four-year period.

Visiting lecturer in growth and innovationGo-to-market and entrepreneurship workshopsTeaching that included business impact

Teach the building blocks of growth

The workshops covered innovation and go-to-market alongside startup and entrepreneurship topics. This combination connected questions about what to build with questions about how an idea reaches people, bringing several parts of company development into the same teaching remit.

Keep impact in the conversation

Impact was also part of the workshop scope. Alongside growth and innovation, it gave the teaching a wider frame for considering what a venture might contribute. The role reflected Jen’s experience across commercial businesses, technical communities, and mission-driven organizations.

Bring practice into a learning environment

The visiting-lecturer appointment ran from January 2021 through December 2024. During that period, teaching sat alongside Jen’s broader advisory practice, connecting an academic setting with a career that spanned go-to-market, brand, partnerships, fundraising, and organizational leadership.
