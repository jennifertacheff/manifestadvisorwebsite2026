Innovation / Strategic partner & senior advisor

Noēsis / HumanConnections.AI

Building a shared agenda for human centered AI

RoleStrategic partner & senior advisor

PeriodJune 2024 – January 2026

FocusInnovation

The future of AI brings technologists, researchers, investors, and policymakers into the same conversation. As a strategic partner and senior advisor to Noēsis, Jen helped create HumanConnections.AI and led workshops and initiatives with Harvard and Cambridge. Her work connected those conversations to organizational development, research collaborations, partnerships, and policy engagement.

Co-creation of HumanConnections.AIHarvard and Cambridge workshopsResearch, partnership, and policy collaboration

Create room for a different conversation

HumanConnections.AI brought together people working on technology and human connection from different disciplines. Jen’s work included shaping workshops and initiatives, with participation spanning universities, AI companies, investors, and public-sector leaders across the United States, United Kingdom, and European Union.

Connect research and policy

The work included the October 2025 Cambridge gathering on AI policy and youth well-being. Co-organized with the Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence, the program connected technology, research, policy, philanthropy, and civil society. Jen was listed as a senior advisor and participant.

Turn dialogue into next steps

Jen’s contribution extended into organizational roadmaps, new research collaborations, strategic partnerships, and policy engagement. She also participated in industry and policy roundtables, including sessions with the UK House of Lords, bringing her experience in growth and convening to questions about human centered AI.
