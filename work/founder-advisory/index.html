Founder & creative advisory

Giving ambitious founders a clearer way to grow

RoleIndividual founder advisory engagements

FocusLeadership

Founder support takes different forms at different stages. Across separate engagements, Jen has helped founders clarify their stories, explore growth opportunities, develop confidence, and move projects forward. Recommendations from Kula Project, Yayble, Huggnote, VR Perspectives, The Broadway Gram, and The Girlfriend Manifesto show how that work adapts to the person and the business.

Founder storytelling and growth opportunitiesCoaching through uncertainty and changeCreative launches and relationship building

Find the story and the opportunity

Kula Project’s Sarah B Sasson credits Manifest with helping her recognize the strength of her own story. Yayble founder Kate Myers describes branding and marketing support that opened up ideas around customer segments, growth opportunities, and additional revenue streams during the company’s early stages.

Give founders a place to think

Huggnote founder Jacqui Meskell’s relationship with Jen began with coaching at SXSW and continued as ongoing support. VR Perspectives founder Myra LalDin describes strategic perspective, interpersonal insight, and an ability to bring clarity during uncertainty. Each account reflects a separate founder relationship.

Help the idea become real

The work also reaches creative businesses. The Broadway Gram credits Jen’s guidance and coaching; The Girlfriend Manifesto’s Ayesha Ophelia describes help refining ideas and getting a project launched. Wellness entrepreneur Shawnte Forrer credits strategy, branding, networking, partnerships, and support for an early public teaching opportunity.

“I had my first coaching session with Jennifer at SXSW and she has been a critical part of our support system ever since.”
