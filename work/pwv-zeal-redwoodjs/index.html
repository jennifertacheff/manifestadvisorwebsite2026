Preston-Werner Ventures / ZEAL / RedwoodJS

Connecting a developer ecosystem around what comes next

RoleFractional CMO / COO

FocusVenture

Developer ecosystems grow through useful technology and the relationships around it. Working with Preston-Werner Ventures and ZEAL in a fractional CMO and COO capacity, Jen supported the visibility of RedwoodJS, developer and founder engagement, sponsorships, and partnerships. The work also included the inaugural RedwoodJS Conference for Builders in Grants Pass, Oregon.

Fractional marketing and operating leadershipDeveloper and founder engagementRedwoodJS conference, sponsorships, and partnerships

Leadership across the ecosystem

The remit combined strategic brand work with operational support. Jen worked across the full-stack framework’s visibility, developer and founder engagement, sponsorships, and partnership integrations. The engagement connected the interests of builders, a software agency, and a venture organization around a shared ecosystem.

Bring the community together

The first RedwoodJS Conference for Builders took place in September 2023. It brought developers, founders, and technology leaders together for talks, workshops, a build competition, and community events. ZEAL co-founder Trever Yarrish specifically credits Jen’s organization and strategic contribution to the conference.

A partner in the company’s growth

The work extended beyond one event. David Price describes Jen as instrumental in shaping PWV, while Trever Yarrish describes an ongoing partnership spanning strategy, operations, relationships, and execution. Together, those accounts reflect a role that connected company development with ecosystem building.

“Jennifer has been instrumental in helping to shape PWV into what it is today. I encourage anyone building a company or growth strategy to reach out to her.”

David PriceGeneral Partner, Preston Werner Ventures (PWV)
