Funds & Fundraising is for fund builders launching a new fund and founders preparing to raise. Manifest Advisors delivers fund positioning, fundraising narratives, ecosystem partnerships, and founder readiness, resulting in a coherent pitch, a prepared leadership team, and stronger relationships. This is strategy and positioning work, not investment advice.

Related case studies: Chabot Space & Science Center, Girls Inc., MotherCoders, Preston-Werner Ventures / ZEAL / RedwoodJS, Jubilee Productions / THE DIVISIBLE, W Fund / SWITCH, Women Who Code. Questions? See the Manifest Advisors FAQ.

How we help

Fund positioning

Give a new fund a clear place in the ecosystem. Articulate what you believe, who you serve, and the value your team brings, then connect that positioning to your brand and communications.

Fundraising narratives

Build a pitch around the opportunity that matters. Refine the business story, clarify the value proposition, and prepare founders to communicate their vision with substance and conviction.

Ecosystem & partnerships

Connect your strategy with the communities and collaborators that can support it. Develop partnership opportunities, strengthen stakeholder relationships, and create programs that give people a reason to participate.

Founder readiness

Work through the leadership and focus behind a successful next step. Coaching gives founders space to prioritize, prepare for important conversations, and build the confidence to lead through uncertainty.
