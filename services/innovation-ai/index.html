GoFundMe / Classy

Bringing new AI and data products to market

Go-to-market advisory

New technology needs a clear place in the market. Jen supported go-to-market work for new AI and data products at GoFundMe and Classy, connecting product direction, innovation, and brand visibility. The engagement brought a growth perspective to how emerging capabilities were introduced and how their place in the product roadmap developed.

The remit focused on new data and AI products. Bringing those capabilities to market connects several kinds of work: product strategy, positioning, and the way a company communicates its innovation. Jen’s contribution sat at that intersection of product and growth.

Noēsis / HumanConnections.AI

Building a shared agenda for human centered AI

Strategic partner & senior advisor

The future of AI brings technologists, researchers, investors, and policymakers into the same conversation. As a strategic partner and senior advisor to Noēsis, Jen helped create HumanConnections.AI and led workshops and initiatives with Harvard and Cambridge. Her work connected those conversations to organizational development, research collaborations, partnerships, and policy engagement.

HumanConnections.AI brought together people working on technology and human connection from different disciplines. Jen’s work included shaping workshops and initiatives, with participation spanning universities, AI companies, investors, and public-sector leaders across the United States, United Kingdom, and European Union.

UC Berkeley Haas

Bringing growth and innovation into the classroom

Visiting lecturer

Jen’s work has also included teaching the disciplines behind company building. As a visiting lecturer at UC Berkeley’s Haas School of Business, she delivered workshops on growth, innovation, go-to-market, startups, entrepreneurship, and impact. The role brought her operating and advisory experience into an educational setting over a four-year period.

The workshops covered innovation and go-to-market alongside startup and entrepreneurship topics. This combination connected questions about what to build with questions about how an idea reaches people, bringing several parts of company development into the same teaching remit.
