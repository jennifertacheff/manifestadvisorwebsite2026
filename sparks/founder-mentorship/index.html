Founder mentorship

A sounding board for the people building next

Founders need room to think alongside the pressure to act. Jen’s mentoring work spans accelerator communities, startup programs, and individual founder relationships. It draws on experience in growth, brand, partnerships, and leadership, with support that can extend from an early coaching conversation into a longer relationship.

Inside the startup ecosystem

Jen serves on SXSW’s advisory board and has sourced and reviewed startups across AI, robotics, enterprise technology, media, and the future of work. She also mentors through Techstars Anywhere and was named among the program’s 2023 All-Star Mentors.

A program for the person leading the business

At W Fund and SWITCH, Jen built and co-led a coaching program for founders and executives. The engagement connected business development and personal leadership, with a partner who had worked across company growth, technical communities, marketing, and organizational change.

Relationships that continue beyond the first conversation

Huggnote founder Jacqui Meskell describes an initial coaching session at SXSW that became ongoing support. Other founder recommendations speak to clearer positioning, confidence, and opportunities for growth. These are individual accounts of advisory relationships, with different starting points and needs.
