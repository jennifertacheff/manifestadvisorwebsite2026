Speaking & convening

Ideas get stronger when people come together

From founder workshops to conversations about AI and society, Jen’s work has often involved bringing the right people into a room. Her experience spans teaching, conference development, startup mentoring, and policy dialogue, connecting practical business questions with the communities that can help move them forward.

Conversations across technology and society

As a strategic partner and senior advisor to Noēsis, Jen helped lead AI workshops and initiatives with Harvard and Cambridge. Her experience also includes industry and policy roundtables, with participants from research, technology, investment, and government.

  • HumanConnections.AI: technology and human connection
  • Cambridge, October 2025: AI policy and youth well-being
  • Industry and policy roundtables, including UK House of Lords sessions

Learning with founders and builders

Jen’s work with the RedwoodJS Conference for Builders connected developer and founder communities through talks, workshops, and shared experiences. At Berkeley Haas, her visiting-lecturer work covered growth, innovation, go-to-market, entrepreneurship, and impact. These were distinct engagements spanning community development and education.

  • RedwoodJS Conference for Builders, 2023
  • UC Berkeley Haas visiting lecturer, 2021–2024
  • SXSW advisory, mentoring, and pitch-coaching work

A history of bringing communities together

Earlier roles included Women Who Code’s CONNECT programs, leadership summits, and global partner events, as well as speaker series and special projects at Chabot Space and Science Center. That history connects contemporary technology conversations with longstanding work in community participation and public programming.
